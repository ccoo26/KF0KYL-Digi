### What's new in 1.2.3

**Click, then Enter**
- **Single-click a line** in Band Activity or Rx Frequency to select it. It gets a green border, and only one line is selected at a time across both windows.
- **Enter** calls the selected station, the same as a double-click. **↑ ↓**, **Page Up** and **Page Down** move the selection. Double-click still works, and **Esc** still halts Tx.
- The selection stays on its line as new decodes come in. A small key hint shows under the window; click its **×** to hide it for good.

**Max transmit power**
- **Settings → Radio → Rig Data → Don't transmit above** sets the most watts you want to send. It's off until you turn it on. Before each transmission KFØKYL Digi reads the radio's power setting over CAT. If it's above your maximum, nothing is transmitted, Tx is turned off and you're told to turn the power down. Handy after a session of 100 W SSB.
- Radios that can't report their power setting are never blocked.

**Fixes**
- The **Settings** window always fits on your screen, with **OK** and **Cancel** visible. Anything that doesn't fit scrolls. (Ubuntu and Windows.)
- Fixed a crash when closing the program while the PSK Reporter **Live** feed was on. That crash could also lose settings changed since the last start.
- **Max tries** are saved the moment you change them.
- Settings are saved first thing when you close the program, and on Linux also when the computer shuts down, restarts or logs out with the program still open.
- The key hint under Band Activity and Rx Frequency is no longer cut off at the bottom.
- Windows come back from being minimized exactly as they were, same size and same place.
